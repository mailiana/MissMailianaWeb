About Miss Mailiana

Grounded in learning. Driven by meaningful change.

My work sits at the intersection of technology, education, business and human potential. I believe digital transformation should be understandable, practical and accessible—not simply a collection of tools.

With a foundation across economics, mathematics, statistics and data, my perspective connects analytical thinking with the people and processes that make change possible.

My long-term vision is to help build stronger African digital capabilities and create more opportunities for people and institutions to participate in the global technology economy.
